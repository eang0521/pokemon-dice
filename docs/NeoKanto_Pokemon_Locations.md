## Notes

- **9 starters** never appear in wild tables — Professor-gift-only, as established from the start.
- **11 species have no wild-encounter row at all**: Hypno, Magnezone, Musharna, Politoed, Poliwrath, Porygon2, Porygon-Z, Rapidash, Slowbro, Vileplume, Volcarona. Every one is reachable by evolving an already-placed lower stage (level-up, stone, or trade per `NeoKanto_Evolution_Guide.md`) — not a gap, just a species that's evolution-only in practice, same as several real Pokémon across the mainline games.
- **Mew** stays genuinely unfixed (no location at all, by design) and **Genesect** is postgame-only, replacing Moltres at Cinnabar Mansion / Geothermal Complex once all 8 gyms are cleared rather than occupying its own separate sum-12 slot.
- This pass caught and fixed 11 species that had *no* wild location and *no* evolution fallback (Aerodactyl, Kabuto, Chatot, Dratini, Girafarig, Klefki, Lunatone, Solrock, Munna, Mr. Mime, Porygon) — those were genuine gaps, patched directly into `NeoKanto_Encounter_Tables.md` via minimal swaps before this index was generated, which is why this doc and that one agree perfectly.
- Built by parsing the actual encounter tables rather than hand-compiling, so if `NeoKanto_Encounter_Tables.md` changes later, this doc should be regenerated from it rather than hand-edited out of sync.
