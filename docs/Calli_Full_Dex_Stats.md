# Calli Region — Full Regional Dex: Die Stats

Every Pokémon's six die faces, derived from its normal base stats (HP, Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, Speed, in that order) divided by 17.5 and rounded to the nearest whole number. Calli doesn't name these as traditional stats — they're just the six faces of the die each Pokémon rolls in battle. **0 is a legal face value.**
