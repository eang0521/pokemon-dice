# Neo Kanto Regional Dex

*The canonical, single source of truth for every Pokémon in Neo Kanto — number, type, habitat, and die-face stats (base stat ÷ 17.5, rounded; 0 is a legal face). Every other Neo Kanto doc keys off these numbers and species names. Grouped by habitat zone (the town each zone's routes/dungeons feed into), following the finalized map in the Neo Kanto Dossier §7.*

*No baby/pre-evolution Pokémon are included (Pichu, Igglybuff, Cleffa, Mime Jr., Elekid, Munchlax, Happiny, Tyrogue, and Smoochum's baby role, plus Magby) — removed per request, along with Hitmontop, whose only path into the dex was through the now-removed Tyrogue.*
