# Hoenn Region — Evolution Guide

Hoenn uses the same **1–20 level scale** as Calli and Neo Kanto. Conversion table (ruleset §10):

| New Lv | Old Lv | New Lv | Old Lv | New Lv | Old Lv | New Lv | Old Lv |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–3 | 6 | 18–21 | 11 | 41–45 | 16 | 68–73 |
| 2 | 4–6 | 7 | 22–25 | 12 | 46–50 | 17 | 74–79 |
| 3 | 7–9 | 8 | 26–30 | 13 | 51–55 | 18 | 80–86 |
| 4 | 10–13 | 9 | 31–35 | 14 | 56–61 | 19 | 87–93 |
| 5 | 14–17 | 10 | 36–40 | 15 | 62–67 | 20 | 94–100 |

Non-level evolutions (stone, trade, friendship) get a "proper level" via the buffer rule (ruleset §11): a base (first-stage) Pokémon's non-level evolution = proper level 1 + 6 = New Lv 7; a non-level evolution from an already-evolved stage = that stage's own proper level + 3. **Exception:** when a split evolution has a level-based sibling, the non-level branch borrows that sibling's actual level instead of the generic buffer (e.g. Nuzleaf borrows Seedot's line's own progression rather than a flat buffer where a level-based alternative exists in the same split).

**This dex is the real, official Pokémon Emerald Hoenn Regional Dex, minus Jirachi, Deoxys, Regirock, Regice, Registeel, and the three baby Pokémon Azurill, Igglybuff, and Pichu (194 species) — nothing was added to it.** The babies were cut by request — their evolved lines (Marill, Jigglypuff, Pikachu) stay in and are simply treated as base species now. See `Hoenn_Dungeons.md` for why each was cut — in short, none had a remaining dungeon to call home after the region's dungeon count was trimmed, and none are obtainable in the source game without either an event or a now-cut ruin dungeon. A handful of evolutions that exist in later generations (Nosepass→Probopass, Girafarig→Farigiraf, Magneton→Magnezone, Kirlia→Gallade, Snorunt→Froslass, Dusclops→Dusknoir, Rhydon→Rhyperior, Roselia→Roserade) target species that were introduced after Generation III and were never added to Emerald's fixed regional dex. Those evolution lines simply stop one stage earlier here than they might in a modern game — this matches how the source game actually behaves, not a simplification made for this project.

## Species with no evolution in this dex

Everything else in the 194-species list is either genuinely single-stage in the source games (Nosepass, Girafarig, Absol, Sableye, Torkoal, Tropius, Skarmory, Relicanth, Corsola, Luvdisc, Lunatone, Solrock, Chimecho, Wobbuffet's line-final Wobbuffet, Heracross, Pinsir, Castform, Kecleon, Spinda, Mawile, Volbeat, Illumise, Plusle, Minun, Delcatty [as the final stage], every Legendary/Mythical) or a final evolutionary stage already reached in the table above. Roselia has no in-dex evolution (Roserade is a Gen 4 addition) and stays single-stage here despite technically having a canon pre-evolution (Budew) and evolution outside this dex's scope.

## Notes

- **Trade evolutions** work the same as Calli's and Neo Kanto's precedent — an in-game trade-kiosk NPC stands in for a second physical copy.
- **Friendship evolutions** (Golbat) trigger on level-up once friendship is high enough, same as always.
- **Wurmple's split** (Silcoon/Cascoon, and consequently Beautifly/Dustox) is randomized by an internal personality value in the source game, not a player choice — this project rolls it randomly the same way, same convention as any other branching evolution (ruleset §11).
- **Sibling-level-borrowing example:** none of Hoenn's non-level splits happen to have a level-based sibling in the same split (Gloom's two branches are both stone-based, Clamperl's two branches are both trade-based), so the buffer rule's exception clause isn't exercised anywhere in this dex — every non-level evolution above uses the plain base+6 / prior+3 buffer.
