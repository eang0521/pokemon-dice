# Calli Region — Elite Four & Champion
### Shoreline City (San Diego)

With Fire and Dark now covered by the Dunebreak and Canyon gyms, the Elite Four's specialties shifted to **Bug, Grass, Rock, and Fighting** — none of which appear among the 8 gyms. Wren and Ivy, formerly the Redwood Town and Vinehaven Town gym leaders, were promoted here rather than replaced outright, so their aces stayed the same even as their rosters got stronger.

**What changed from the last version:**
- **Diego (Fire) and Selena (Dark) are retired** — those types now belong to Solana's and Cruz's gyms, so keeping them here would've meant fighting the same type twice in one playthrough.
- **Wren and Ivy moved up from gym leader to Elite Four**, keeping their signature aces (Beedrill, Vileplume) for continuity, with the rest of their rosters upgraded to final-evolution, no-tiering teams befitting the Elite Four.
- **Marcus, Cortez, and Champion Sierra are unchanged** — none of their types or Pokémon overlapped with the new gym assignments.
