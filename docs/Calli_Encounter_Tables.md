# Calli Region — Wild Encounter Tables

## How Encounters Work

Roll 2d6, then reroll any die showing above the current threshold:

| Badges | Threshold | Dice show | Most common sum | P(that sum) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2 | 1–2 | 3 | 50.0% |
| 1–2 | 3 | 1–3 | 4 | 33.3% |
| 3–4 | 4 | 1–4 | 5 | 25.0% |
| 5–6 | 5 | 1–5 | 6 | 20.0% |
| 7–8 | 6 | 1–6 | 7 | 16.7% |

Each location has one fixed table covering sums 2–12. Early-badge players can only roll the low end (max sum = 2× current threshold), so high-numbered slots are naturally locked away until later — no table changes, just which sums are reachable.

## Full Probability Reference

| Sum | 0 badges | 1–2 badges | 3–4 badges | 5–6 badges | 7–8 badges |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 25.00% | 11.11% | 6.25% | 4.00% | 2.78% |
| 3 | 50.00% | 22.22% | 12.50% | 8.00% | 5.56% |
| 4 | 25.00% | 33.33% | 18.75% | 12.00% | 8.33% |
| 5 | — | 22.22% | 25.00% | 16.00% | 11.11% |
| 6 | — | 11.11% | 18.75% | 20.00% | 13.89% |
| 7 | — | — | 12.50% | 16.00% | 16.67% |
| 8 | — | — | 6.25% | 12.00% | 13.89% |
| 9 | — | — | — | 8.00% | 11.11% |
| 10 | — | — | — | 4.00% | 8.33% |
| 11 | — | — | — | — | 5.56% |
| 12 | — | — | — | — | 2.78% |

**Note on rarity:** at 7–8 badges, sum 12 is only a 1/36 (2.78%) roll — genuinely rare, which is why it's reserved for Legendaries/Mythicals. Every other high-value Pokémon placed at slot 11 or 12 elsewhere in these tables also has an easier-to-catch pre-evolution somewhere else in the region, so nothing is *gated* behind that 2.78% roll — it's just the fastest way to get one already evolved.

**Rarity ordering:** within every table, base-stage Pokémon of multi-stage lines always occupy lower (more common) sums than any evolved Pokémon from a *different* line in that same table — evolved forms are never accidentally more common than an unrelated base form. Single-stage "oddity" Pokémon (Skarmory, Absol, Sableye, Aerodactyl) sit at higher, rarer sums befitting their status as rare finds even though they never evolve.

## Design Notes

- **All 7 Legendaries/Mythicals sit at slot 12** of their designated dungeon — Redwood Grove Depths → Celebi, Delta Hydro Station → Thundurus, Golden Vein Mine → Landorus, Salt Flat Ruins → Heatran, Boulder Maze → Jirachi, Cliffside Grotto → Tornadus, Kelp Forest Trench → Suicune. Sum 12 is only reachable at 7+ badges, so this is naturally a postgame-only encounter.
- **Slot 2 and slot 4 are different species on every table.**
- **Every regional dex Pokémon except the 3 starter lines is reachable somewhere in the wild.**
- **Rarity is now internally consistent** — every table was audited so no evolved Pokémon is ever more common than an unrelated base-stage Pokémon in the same table.
