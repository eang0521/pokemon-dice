# Calli Region — Route Trainers (Revised v2)

## What changed this pass

Both tables had the probability curve backwards. On 2d6, **7 is the most likely roll, and 2/12 are tied for least likely** — the previous version put the most "common/basic" flavor picks (Youngster, Lass, a plain Bidoof on Ranger's table) at 2 and 3, which are actually *rare* rolls. Fixed by re-sorting everything so the most typical pick for a class sits at 7, and the most unusual/rare pick sits at the 2/12 extremes — for both which trainer class you meet, and which Pokémon that class sends out.

## The 11 Trainer Classes

| Roll | Class | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Ranger | On-duty, patrolling official territory — not just any wanderer. |
| 3 | Backpacker | On the road indefinitely — the most eclectic, unpredictable team of any class. |
| 4 | Hiker | Mountain-and-mineshaft regular, favors sturdy Rock/Ground types. |
| 5 | Bug Catcher | Net in hand, turns over every rock and log along the trail. |
| 6 | Lass | Soft-spoken, favors cute and gentle-looking Pokémon. |
| 7 | Youngster | Everyday route kid — whatever they caught first, still using it. |
| 8 | Camper | Sets up wherever the trail allows, works with whatever wanders into camp. |
| 9 | Bird Keeper | Trains anything that takes to the air, feathered or not. |
| 10 | Cyclist | Built a team for speed, wants a battle over before you can blink. |
| 11 | Black Belt | Scrappy, tough, and stronger than they look. |
| 12 | Ace Trainer | The real test. A showcase team, one flashy pick from nearly every corner of the dex. |

*7 (Youngster) is the single most probable roll. 2 (Ranger) and 12 (Ace Trainer) are tied for rarest — fitting, since both are meant to feel like a genuinely unusual encounter rather than an everyday one.*

## Team Size & Level

Team size follows the tiered breakpoints used everywhere else in Calli. **Level does not** — it's a flat "3 + your exact current badge count," changing every single badge, not just at tier breaks.

| Badges | Team size | Pokémon level |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 1 | 3 |
| 1 | 2 | 4 |
| 2 | 2 | 5 |
| 3 | 3 | 6 |
| 4 | 3 | 7 |
| 5 | 4 | 8 |
| 6 | 4 | 9 |
| 7 | 5 | 10 |
| 8 | 5 | 11 |

Every Pokémon on a given trainer's team is the same level — no per-Pokémon level roll like wild encounters have.


---

## Rolling a Trainer's Team

Once you know the class, roll 2d6 **once per Pokémon** the trainer fields. Every slot on a class's table is still a different Pokémon line — no repeats within a class — but now **the line at 7 is the class's most typical/iconic pick**, and the lines at 2 and 12 are its rarest, most unusual picks.

Use the trainer's flat level (3 + badges) to pick the correct stage of whichever line gets rolled — downgrade toward the base form if the level's too low, same rule as wild encounters.
