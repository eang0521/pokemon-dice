# Hoenn Trainer Classes

*All 11 classes below are terrain-neutral by design — none of them require specific terrain to make sense as a concept, so any of them can plausibly turn up on any route or dungeon in Hoenn regardless of biome. Roll the class on a plain 2d6 (no reroll-above-threshold; unlike wild encounter rarity, trainer type doesn't scale with badge count). Every table lists each line's **base (unevolved, lowest-stage) species only** — the trainer's actual battle stage is resolved elsewhere from level, not stored here. No evolutionary line repeats within a single class's own table; the same line reappearing across different classes is expected and intentional.*

## The 11 Trainer Classes

| Roll | Class | Flavor |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Team Aqua/Magma Grunt | Low-ranking criminal foot soldier — Aqua's sea-dogs or Magma's desert diggers. |

| 4 | Cyclist | Built for speed — wants the battle over before the pedals stop. |
| 5 | Hiker | Mountain-path regular, favors sturdy Rock/Ground types. |
| 6 | Lass | Soft-spoken, favors cute and whimsical-looking Pokémon. |
| 7 | Youngster | Everyday route kid — first Pokémon caught, still the starting lineup. |
| 8 | Bug Catcher | Net always out — turns over every log and rock for bugs and small critters. |
| 9 | Camper | Sets up wherever the trail allows, fields whatever nature offers. |
| 10 | Bird Keeper | Trains anything that takes to the air. |
| 11 | Psychic | Studies the mind, and the strange, in equal measure. |
| 12 | Ace Trainer | The real test — a showcase team, one flashy pick from nearly every corner of the dex. |

*7 (Youngster) is the single most probable roll. 2 (Grunt) and 12 (Ace Trainer) are tied for rarest — fitting, since both are meant to feel like a genuinely unusual encounter. Within each class's own 11-slot species table below, the same logic applies: sum 7 holds that class's single most typical pick, and the 2/12 extremes hold its most surprising or unusual ones.*
