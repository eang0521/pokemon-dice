# Hoenn Region — Wild Encounter Tables

## How Encounters Work

Roll 2d6, then reroll any die showing above the current threshold:

| Badges | Threshold | Dice show | Most common sum | P(that sum) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 2 | 1–2 | 3 | 50.0% |
| 1–2 | 3 | 1–3 | 4 | 33.3% |
| 3–4 | 4 | 1–4 | 5 | 25.0% |
| 5–6 | 5 | 1–5 | 6 | 20.0% |
| 7–8 | 6 | 1–6 | 7 | 16.7% |

Each location has one fixed table covering sums 2–12. Early-badge players can only roll the low end (max sum = 2× current threshold), so high-numbered slots are naturally locked away until later — no table changes, just which sums are reachable. Routes made of multiple lettered map segments (e.g. `105a`/`105b`) all share the one table listed under their route number. **Routes 124–127 share one table, and Routes 128–134 share a second** — see the notes on each.
