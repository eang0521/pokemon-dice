# Neo Kanto Gym Leaders

*All 8 gyms are challengeable in any order, from the start — see the Dossier §2 for why that's a rule change from the original games, not just a suggestion. Every leader uses the same 5-slot structure: slots 1 & 2 are always present, slot 3 unlocks once you've already claimed 2+ badges, slot 4 at 4+, slot 5 at 6+. Send-out order is always 5, 2, 3, 4, 1 — the newest addition goes out first, the Ace last. Levels: slots 1 & 4 = badges + 5; slots 2 & 3 = badges + 4; slot 5 = badges + 3, using your exact badge count at the time of the fight, not the tier. Species shown below are resolved at the low end of each tier's badge range — the actual tool computes the exact stage live from your precise badge count. For die-face stats on any Pokémon named below, look it up by name in `NeoKanto_Regional_Dex.md` — same numbers, no separate stats table here.*

## 1. Terra — Pewter City — Rock Gym

*Brock's former student, now running the quarry gym herself. Doesn't waste time — every one of her Pokémon hits like the rock it's made of.*

**Core:** Geodude (Ace) and Onix are always in the fight; Kabuto, Rhyhorn, and Aerodactyl join as badges accumulate.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Onix, **Geodude** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Onix, +Kabuto, **Graveler** |
| 5th/6th gym | Steelix, Kabuto, +Rhyhorn, **Graveler** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Aerodactyl, Steelix, Kabutops, Rhydon, **Golem** |

**Evolutions:** Geodude→Graveler (Level 7) →Golem (Trade, Level 10); Onix→Steelix (Trade holding Metal Coat, Level 7); Kabuto→Kabutops (Level 10); Rhyhorn→Rhydon (Level 11).

## 2. Marina — Cerulean City — Water Gym

*Misty's successor, trained in the city's public aquarium tanks rather than the old gym pool — the team reflects it, leaning toward the showier end of Water-type battling.*

**Core:** Staryu (Ace) and Psyduck anchor the early fights; Poliwag, Shellder, and Horsea round it out.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Psyduck, **Staryu** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Psyduck, +Poliwag, **Starmie** |
| 5th/6th gym | Psyduck, Poliwhirl, +Cloyster, **Starmie** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Seadra, Golduck, Poliwrath, Cloyster, **Starmie** |

**Evolutions:** Staryu→Starmie (Water Stone, Level 7); Psyduck→Golduck (Level 9); Poliwag→Poliwhirl (Level 7) →Poliwrath (Water Stone, Level 10); Shellder→Cloyster (Water Stone, Level 7); Horsea→Seadra (Level 9).

## 3. Ferris — Vermilion City — Steel Gym

*Runs the gym out of a converted shipping-container office on the megaport docks. Every Pokémon on the team doubles as something the port itself actually uses.*

**Core:** Magnemite (Ace) and Skarmory hold the line early; Mawile, Joltik, and Klefki join later.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Skarmory, **Magnemite** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Skarmory, +Mawile, **Magnemite** |
| 5th/6th gym | Skarmory, Mawile, +Joltik, **Magneton** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Klefki, Skarmory, Mawile, Galvantula, **Magnezone** |

**Evolutions:** Magnemite→Magneton (Level 8) →Magnezone (special magnetic site, Level 11); Joltik→Galvantula (Level 10).

---

## 4. Ophelia — Lavender Town — Ghost Gym

*Caretaker of the reopened Pokémon Tower. The gym battle doubles as an unofficial tour — she introduces each Pokémon by name, like she's showing off old friends.*

**Core:** Duskull (Ace) and Gastly are the earliest fights; Litwick, Marowak, and Sableye join later. Marowak is the one non-Ghost pick on the team — a deliberate nod to the Tower's older ghost-story roots rather than a coverage gap.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Gastly, **Duskull** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Gastly, +Litwick, **Duskull** |
| 5th/6th gym | Haunter, Litwick, +Marowak, **Duskull** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Sableye, Gengar, Litwick, Marowak, **Dusclops** |

**Evolutions:** Duskull→Dusclops (Level 10) →Dusknoir (Trade holding Reaper Cloth, Level 13); Gastly→Haunter (Level 7) →Gengar (Trade, Level 10); Litwick→Lampent (Level 11) →Chandelure (Trade holding Dusk Stone, Level 14); Cubone→Marowak (Level 8).

*Note: Duskull's Ace never quite reaches Dusknoir within the gym's own level range (Level 13 needs 8+ effective badges beyond what a gym fight provides) — same texture as any gym leader whose signature line outgrows what a regular challenge can show off.*

## 5. Fern — Celadon City — Grass Gym

*Runs the gym out of the Department Store's rooftop greenhouse district. Doesn't so much "battle" as let her team's territory speak for itself.*

**Core:** Bellsprout (Ace) and Oddish start things off; Exeggcute, Tangela, and Cottonee join as the fight escalates.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Oddish, **Bellsprout** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Gloom, +Exeggcute, **Weepinbell** |
| 5th/6th gym | Gloom, Exeggutor, +Tangela, **Victreebel** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Whimsicott, Vileplume, Exeggutor, Tangrowth, **Victreebel** |

**Evolutions:** Bellsprout→Weepinbell (Level 6) →Victreebel (Leaf Stone, Level 9); Oddish→Gloom (Level 6) →Vileplume (Leaf Stone, Level 9); Exeggcute→Exeggutor (Leaf Stone, Level 7); Tangela→Tangrowth (Level 10); Cottonee→Whimsicott (Sun Stone, Level 7).

## 6. Kai — Fuchsia City — Bug Gym

*Grew up volunteering at the Kanto Wildlife Preserve before taking the gym. Every Pokémon on the team is something you could, in theory, also just meet on a preserve tour.*

**Core:** Scyther (Ace) and Pinsir are the earliest fights; Larvesta, Illumise, and Volbeat join later.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Pinsir, **Scyther** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Pinsir, +Larvesta, **Scizor** |
| 5th/6th gym | Pinsir, Larvesta, +Illumise, **Scizor** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Volbeat, Pinsir, Larvesta, Illumise, **Scizor** |

**Evolutions:** Scyther→Scizor (Trade holding Metal Coat, Level 7); Larvesta→Volcarona (Level 14, not reached within the gym's own range — same texture as Ophelia's Dusknoir).

## 7. Ignis — Cinnabar Island — Fire Gym

*Runs the gym out of the rebuilt geothermal research complex — half battle, half live demonstration of what thirty years of volcanic-science funding can do.*

**Core:** Growlithe (Ace) and Vulpix anchor the early fights; Magmar, Ponyta, and Torkoal join later.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Vulpix, **Growlithe** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Vulpix, +Magmar, **Arcanine** |
| 5th/6th gym | Ninetales, Magmortar, +Ponyta, **Arcanine** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Torkoal, Ninetales, Magmortar, Rapidash, **Arcanine** |

**Evolutions:** Growlithe→Arcanine (Fire Stone, Level 7); Vulpix→Ninetales (Fire Stone, Level 7); Magmar→Magmortar (Trade holding Magmarizer, Level 7); Ponyta→Rapidash (Level 10).

---

## 8. Boreas — Viridian City — Ice Gym

*Runs the gym right at the mouth of Route 26 and Icefall Pass — the newest gym identity in Neo Kanto, and the most literal: her team is exactly what's living twenty minutes up the mountain.*

**Core:** Snover (Ace) and Seel hold the early fights; Jynx, Delibird, and Lapras join later.

| Battled as your... | Team |
|---|---|
| 1st/2nd gym | Seel, **Snover** |
| 3rd/4th gym | Seel, +Jynx, **Snover** |
| 5th/6th gym | Seel, Jynx, +Delibird, **Snover** |
| 7th/8th gym | +Lapras, Dewgong, Jynx, Delibird, **Abomasnow** |

**Evolutions:** Snover→Abomasnow (Level 10); Seel→Dewgong (Level 9).
