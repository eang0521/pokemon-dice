Pokemon Data

Pick any two Pokemon (real species autofill their type and stats -- everything stays editable, so you can also build a fully custom one, or load one straight from My Pokemon), assign bonus points per stat -- their total becomes that Pokemon's effective level -- and an attack type. Each side rolls to pick one of its six stats; type effectiveness changes how many picks are rolled and whether the best or worst is kept, and STAB adds +1. Higher result wins the exchange; a tie means both sides reroll, so the odds below are the final resolved odds -- not a per-roll snapshot. See Dice_Region_Ruleset.md §14 for the full mechanic. Base stats sourced from PokemonDB (pokemon_base_stats.json).
